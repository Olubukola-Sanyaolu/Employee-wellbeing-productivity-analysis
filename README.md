# Employee-wellbeing-productivity-analysis
A Power BI project analyzing employee well-being, stress levels, sleep patterns, and productivity metrics to uncover insights and actionable recommendations for improving work-life balance and performance across departments.
##  Business Problem

Many organisations measure output but miss the hidden costs of declining employee well-being: rising stress, poor work-life balance, and sleep disruption can quietly reduce productivity, increase errors, and raise turnover risk. This project addresses the lack of a unified, data-driven view that links **well-being metrics** (stress, WLB, sleep) to **productivity outcomes** (tasks completed, deadlines met, completion rate) to help HR and leadership make targeted, measurable interventions.

---

##  Project Objectives

### Primary Objective
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard that surfaces actionable insights connecting employee well-being with productivity — enabling HR and team leads to identify high-risk groups, quantify the business impact of poor well-being, and implement targeted interventions.

### Secondary Objectives
- Provide department- and role-level visibility into stress, WLB, and sleep patterns.
- Quantify how changes in well-being (e.g., sleep hours, WLB) affect productivity KPIs.
- Recommend prioritized, data-backed interventions to reduce burnout and improve task completion and retention.

##  DASHBOARD 1: EMPLOYEE WELL-BEING OVERVIEW


<img width="607" height="383" alt="wlb1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b092f545-8079-42b1-824b-5cce9a611589" />

###  Dashboard Focus
High-level summary of employee wellness indicators — tracking **Stress Level**, **Work-Life Balance (WLB)**, and **Sleep Hours** trends across months, departments, gender, and age groups.

---

###  Insights

1. **Stress Level Trend**
   - The **average stress level** is **5.96**, with a **5.4% month-over-month increase**.
   - Stress levels **peak around February and December**, aligning with reporting and project deadlines.
   - The **mid-year period (May–August)** records relatively lower stress levels.

2. **Work-Life Balance (WLB)**
   - The **average WLB score is 1.99**, showing a **3.6% decline MoM**.
   - Marketing shows the **best balance (≈3.0)**, while HR and Finance departments have the **lowest scores (≈1.8–2.0)**.
   - Low WLB scores correlate with higher stress levels.

3. **Sleep Patterns**
   - Employees average **7.01 hours of sleep**, improving by **1.2% MoM**.
   - Sleep hours are lowest mid-year and recover around December.

4. **Gender Distribution**
   - Equal gender distribution (**50% male, 50% female**).
   - WLB scores are balanced across genders, suggesting equitable work distribution.

5. **Age and Stress Distribution**
   - **40–49 age group** shows the **highest average stress (≈6.2)**.
   - **20–29** and **30–39** groups maintain moderate stress.
   - **50+ group** remains stable, likely due to experience in stress management.

---

###  Recommendations

| Focus Area | Actionable Strategy | Quantified Impact |
|-------------|--------------------|------------------|
| **Stress Reduction** | Organize monthly stress management & mindfulness sessions, especially before peak months (Feb & Dec). | Reduce stress levels by **10–15%** within 3 months. |
| **Work-Life Balance** | Introduce flexible working hours and hybrid models for HR & Finance departments. | Improve WLB by **+8% MoM**. |
| **Sleep & Wellness** | Provide wellness incentives (fitness & sleep app rewards). | Expected **5–7% improvement** in productivity. |
| **Age-Specific Interventions** | Provide managerial support programs for 40–49 age group focusing on delegation & workload planning. | Expected **20% stress reduction** in that age group. |
| **Continuous Monitoring** | Deploy monthly well-being pulse surveys for early burnout detection. | Improves HR responsiveness by **30–40%**. |

###  Insights

1. **Finance Department**
   - Consistently high stress levels (**6.0–6.7**).
   - Low WLB averages (≈1–2), particularly among **Managers**.
   - Despite low WLB, employees maintain steady sleep hours (6.7–7.2 hrs).

2. **Role-Level Trends**
   - **Junior-level** employees show higher stress variability but balanced sleep (≈7 hrs).
   - **Managers** show **lowest WLB** and **highest stress levels**, suggesting leadership strain.
   - **Mid-level** roles exhibit moderate stress but inconsistent sleep.

3. **Stress vs. Sleep Correlation**
   - Employees with **>6 stress level** average **6.5–6.8 hrs of sleep**.
   - Those below **5.8 stress level** sleep **>7 hours**, showing an inverse relationship.

4. **Work-Life Balance KPI**
   - ~**60% of employees** have **Low or Moderate WLB** scores.
   - Only ~**25–30%** maintain **High WLB**.

---

###  Recommendations

| Focus Area | Actionable Strategy | Quantified Impact |
|-------------|--------------------|------------------|
| **Manager Support** | Implement leadership resilience training and encourage task delegation. | Expected **15% stress drop** among managers. |
| **Junior Staff Engagement** | Introduce mentorship programs and team-building sessions. | Reduce stress variability by **10%**. |
| **WLB Improvement** | Apply “No-meeting Fridays” and dedicated focus hours. | Increase WLB from **1.99 → 2.5**. |
| **Rest Recovery** | Provide mandatory rest breaks during long projects. | Expected **7% improvement** in sleep hours. |
| **Continuous Feedback** | Enable real-time HR dashboards to track WLB & stress trends. | Increases intervention efficiency by **~25%**. |

##  DASHBOARD 3: EMPLOYEE PRODUCTIVITY

### Dashboard Focus
Examines the relationship between **employee well-being (stress, WLB, sleep)** and **productivity metrics (deadlines met, tasks completed, completion rate)** across departments.

---

###  Insights

1. **Overall Productivity**
   - Average deadlines met: **85.9%**  
   - Task completion rate: **100%** for key departments (Finance, Sales, IT, Marketing).  
   - Indicates strong work ethic but rising stress levels risk sustainability.

2. **Department Comparison**
   - **Marketing:** Highest productivity (86.1%) and strong WLB.  
   - **Sales & Finance:** Excellent task delivery but **high stress** and **low WLB**.  
   - **HR:** Slightly lower delivery (84.6%) but better emotional well-being.  
   - **IT:** Balanced delivery with healthy rest habits.

3. **Sleep Tracker**
   - Average sleep: **7 hours** — correlated with high task completion.  
   - Employees maintaining consistent rest show higher reliability.

4. **Stress Impact on Productivity**
   - Departments with stress >6.0 experience **3–5% lower deadlines met %**.  
   - Overworking leads to diminishing performance returns.

---

### Recommendations

| Focus Area | Actionable Strategy | Quantified Impact |
|-------------|--------------------|------------------|
| **Balanced Workload** | Adjust task distribution for Finance & Sales teams. | Increase task completion by **+5%**, reduce stress by **10%**. |
| **Wellness KPIs** | Integrate well-being metrics into performance reviews. | Boost engagement and retention by **12–15%**. |
| **Collaboration** | Encourage cross-department support between HR & Finance during peak cycles. | Reduce stress spikes by **~8%**. |
| **Sleep Optimization** | Launch “Sleep Challenge” HR campaign to promote rest consistency. | Improve completion rates by **3–4%**. |
| **Automation & Efficiency** | Use task management tools to streamline workload. | Raise deadline compliance from **85.9% → 89–90%**. |

---

##  Overall Conclusion

- **Stress levels (5.96)** are rising despite steady productivity — a sign of **sustained overexertion**.  
- **Work-life balance (1.99)** remains the weakest metric and demands HR focus.  
- Departments maintaining **7h sleep** and **moderate stress** achieve **better consistency**.  
- Promoting employee wellness directly enhances long-term productivity and retention.
